life of a starfish

Starfish are amazing sea animals. They live in the ocean and can be found in many different coulours and sizes. They move slowly along the ocean floor. Starfish are also called sea stars because of their unique shape. Most starfish have five arms, but some species have more. They use their small tube feet to move and find food. Starfish can live in different ocean environments. One interesting fact about starfish is that they can regrow lost arms. They are an important part of marine life.

facts about starfish

  • they have five arms.
  • they move slowly
